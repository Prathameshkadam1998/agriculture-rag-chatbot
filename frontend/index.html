How an answer is made

  1. Search twiceMeaning search (FAISS) and keyword search (BM25) each pull their best 20 passages.
  2. FuseReciprocal Rank Fusion merges both lists into one.
  3. RerankA cross-encoder reads every candidate against your question and re-scores it.
  4. AnswerThe reply is written only from the top sources, which you can open and check.
